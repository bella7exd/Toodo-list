# 🌸 Toodo

A beautiful, theme-based task manager built with Flutter. Designed for those who want their workspace to look as good as it functions, this app brings a soft, aesthetic touch to your daily to-do lists, making productivity feel less like a chore and more like organizing digital stationery.

## ✨ Features

*   🎨 **Custom Aesthetic Themes:** Choose from beautifully crafted pastel palettes (Mint, Blossom, Lavender, Peach, etc.) to customize the vibe of every task group.
*   📁 **Smart Task Groups:** Categorize your life. Keep your study notes, personal goals, and daily chores in separate, easy-to-manage cards.
*   📅 **Dynamic Calendar Tracking:** Effortlessly look back at the last 30 days or plan for the future with a seamless, custom-built interactive calendar view.
*   ✅ **Satisfying Progress:** Watch your daily goals come to life with dynamic circular progress bars and completion trackers.
*   💾 **Local Storage:** Lightning-fast, offline-first data persistence using Shared Preferences.
*   📱 **Responsive UI:** Smooth animations, custom dot-pattern backgrounds, and glassmorphism elements designed specifically for a modern, Gen Z aesthetic.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Framework:** [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/)
*   **Language:** Dart
*   **Data Persistence:** `shared_preferences`
*   **Typography:** `google_fonts` (Nunito)
*   **Date Formatting:** `intl`
